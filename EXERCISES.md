## 1. Push & Chest Movements

### 1.1 Barbell Bench Press
- **ID**: `bench_press` &bull; **Category**: Chest &bull; **Equipment**: Olympic Barbell
- **Primary Agonist**: Pectoralis Major (Sternal & Costal Heads)
- **Synergists**: Anterior Deltoid, Triceps Brachii, Serratus Anterior
- **Prescription**: 3 Sets &times; 6–10 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 150s &bull; **Tempo**: `3-0-1-0`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Lie flat with eyes aligned directly beneath the racked barbell.
  2. Depress and retract scapulae, creating a stable upper-back shelf and natural lumbar arch.
  3. Plant feet flat on the floor for leg drive. Grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder width.
  4. Lower the bar smoothly over 3 seconds to touch the lower-mid sternum line.
  5. Press forcefully upward without losing scapular retraction.
- **Form Cues**: "Tuck elbows at ~45°–60°" &bull; "Drive floor with heels" &bull; "Keep wrists stacked vertically".
- **Common Mistakes**: Bouncing off the ribcage &bull; Flaring elbows out to 90° &bull; Lifting glutes off bench.
- **Safety**: Always utilize safety pins or a competent spotter when handling working sets.

---

### 1.2 Incline Dumbbell Press
- **ID**: `incline_db_press` &bull; **Category**: Chest &bull; **Equipment**: Dumbbells
- **Primary Agonist**: Clavicular Pectoralis (Upper Chest)
- **Synergists**: Anterior Deltoid, Triceps Brachii
- **Prescription**: 3 Sets &times; 8–12 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 120s &bull; **Tempo**: `3-0-1-0`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Position an adjustable bench to a 30° incline (angles $\ge 45^\circ$ recruit excessive front delts).
  2. Kick dumbbells up to shoulder level using knees; retract shoulder blades into pad.
  3. Lower dumbbells outward and down until achieving a deep clavicular stretch.
  4. Press in a slight converging inward arc without clinking dumbbells together at top.
- **Form Cues**: "Maintain 30° bench angle" &bull; "Chest proud at all times" &bull; "Control the bottom stretch".
- **Common Mistakes**: Setting bench too steep &bull; Cutting depth short &bull; Excessively flaring elbows.

---

### 1.3 Machine Chest Press
- **ID**: `machine_chest_press` &bull; **Category**: Chest &bull; **Equipment**: Selectorized Machine
- **Primary Agonist**: Pectoralis Major
- **Synergists**: Anterior Deltoids, Triceps
- **Prescription**: 2 Sets &times; 10–12 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 90s &bull; **Tempo**: `2-1-1-0`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Adjust seat height so handles line up with mid-chest line.
  2. Keep shoulders pinned firmly against the backrest pad.
  3. Drive handles smoothly forward to arm extension without slamming elbow joints.
- **Form Cues**: "Pin shoulders back" &bull; "Pause 1s at full contraction".

---

### 1.4 Cable Chest Fly
- **ID**: `cable_fly` (`cable_chest_fly`) &bull; **Category**: Chest &bull; **Equipment**: Dual Cable Pulleys
- **Primary Agonist**: Sternal & Costal Pectoralis
- **Synergists**: Anterior Deltoid
- **Prescription**: 2 Sets &times; 12–15 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 90s &bull; **Tempo**: `3-1-1-0`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Set pulleys at chest level; adopt a split stance with a slight forward torso lean.
  2. Maintain a fixed 15° soft bend in the elbows.
  3. Bring handles together in an expansive hugging arc until hands meet at midline.
- **Form Cues**: "Imagine hugging a wide tree trunk" &bull; "Squeeze chest hard at midline".

---

### 1.5 Dumbbell Lateral Raise
- **ID**: `lateral_raise` (`db_lateral_raise`) &bull; **Category**: Shoulders &bull; **Equipment**: Dumbbells
- **Primary Agonist**: Lateral Deltoid
- **Synergists**: Upper Trapezius, Supraspinatus
- **Prescription**: 4 Sets &times; 12–15 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 60s &bull; **Tempo**: `2-0-1-1`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Stand tall, hinge forward 10° at hips to align lateral delt in the scapular plane.
  2. Raise arms outward 30° in front of the coronal plane until parallel with floor.
  3. Lead with elbows, pause briefly at top, and control descent over 2 seconds.
- **Form Cues**: "Lead with elbows" &bull; "Raise in scapular plane" &bull; "Don't swing with hips".

---

### 1.6 Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press
- **ID**: `shoulder_press` (`seated_db_shoulder_press`) &bull; **Category**: Shoulders &bull; **Equipment**: Dumbbells
- **Primary Agonist**: Anterior Deltoid
- **Synergists**: Lateral Deltoid, Triceps Brachii, Upper Trapezius
- **Prescription**: 2 Sets &times; 8–12 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 120s &bull; **Tempo**: `3-0-1-0`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Set upright bench to 75°–80° (prevents subacromial shoulder impingement).
  2. Bring dumbbells to ear level with elbows positioned slightly forward.
  3. Press vertically overhead to full arm extension without locking out violently.
- **Form Cues**: "75° bench angle, not 90°" &bull; "Core braced tight" &bull; "Elbows tracked in front of ears".

---

### 1.7 Cable Triceps Pushdown
- **ID**: `triceps_pushdown` (`cable_triceps_pushdown`) &bull; **Category**: Arms &bull; **Equipment**: High Cable Pulley
- **Primary Agonist**: Triceps Brachii (Lateral & Medial Heads)
- **Synergists**: Anconeus, Forearms
- **Prescription**: 3 Sets &times; 10–15 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 90s &bull; **Tempo**: `3-0-1-1`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Attach straight bar or rope to high pulley.
  2. Glue elbows to sides of ribcage; hinge torso forward slightly.
  3. Push downward to full elbow extension; hold contraction for 1 full second.
- **Form Cues**: "Pin elbows like fixed hinges" &bull; "Spread rope or snap straight bar down".

---

### 1.8 Overhead Cable Triceps Extension
- **ID**: `overhead_triceps_ext` (`overhead_cable_triceps_ext`) &bull; **Category**: Arms &bull; **Equipment**: Cable
- **Primary Agonist**: Triceps Brachii (Long Head)
- **Synergists**: Triceps Lateral Head
- **Prescription**: 2 Sets &times; 10–15 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 90s &bull; **Tempo**: `3-0-1-0`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Face away from cable station with rope attachment held behind head.
  2. Stagger feet in athletic split stance; keep upper arms fixed beside ears.
  3. Extend forearms forward and overhead, loading the long head under deep stretch.
- **Form Cues**: "Feel deep stretch behind the head" &bull; "Keep elbows pointed forward".

## 2. Pull & Back Movements

### 2.1 Wide Grip Lat Pulldown
- **ID**: `lat_pulldown` &bull; **Category**: Back &bull; **Equipment**: Lat Pulldown Station
- **Primary Agonist**: Latissimus Dorsi
- **Synergists**: Biceps Brachii, Brachialis, Rear Deltoids, Teres Major
- **Prescription**: 3 Sets &times; 8–12 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 120s &bull; **Tempo**: `3-1-1-0`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Lock thighs firmly under pads. Grip bar slightly wider than shoulder width with overhand grip.
  2. Lean back slightly (~10°–15°) with chest proud.
  3. Pull by depressing scapulae and driving elbows down towards back hip pockets.
  4. Touch bar to upper chest/clavicle line; pause for 1 second.
- **Form Cues**: "Drive elbows to back pockets" &bull; "Never pull behind the neck".

---

### 2.2 Chest Supported T-Bar / DB Row
- **ID**: `chest_supported_row` &bull; **Category**: Back &bull; **Equipment**: Incline Bench / Machine
- **Primary Agonist**: Rhomboids, Middle Trapezius, Upper Lats
- **Synergists**: Posterior Deltoids, Biceps
- **Prescription**: 3 Sets &times; 8–12 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 120s &bull; **Tempo**: `3-1-1-0`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Lie chest down on 30°–45° incline bench pad with arms hanging fully extended.
  2. Pull weights up by driving elbows back and retracting shoulder blades together.
  3. Squeeze mid-back hard for 1 second at top before lowering with full control.
- **Form Cues**: "Sternum stays on pad (zero lower back cheating)" &bull; "Squeeze shoulder blades together".

---

### 2.3 Seated Cable Row
- **ID**: `seated_cable_row` &bull; **Category**: Back &bull; **Equipment**: Low Cable Pulley
- **Primary Agonist**: Latissimus Dorsi, Mid Traps
- **Synergists**: Biceps, Posterior Deltoid
- **Prescription**: 2–3 Sets &times; 8–12 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 90s–120s &bull; **Tempo**: `2-1-1-0`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Sit upright with soft bend in knees, bracing feet against footrests.
  2. Grasp V-handle with erect neutral spine.
  3. Row handle towards lower abdomen/navel, squeezing elbows tight to ribs.
- **Form Cues**: "Upright neutral spine (no rocking like a rowboat)" &bull; "Pull into belly button".

---

## 3. Legs & Lower Body Movements

### 3.1 45-Degree Incline Leg Press
- **ID**: `leg_press` &bull; **Category**: Legs &bull; **Equipment**: Incline Leg Press Sled
- **Primary Agonist**: Quadriceps (Rectus Femoris, Vastus Lateralis/Medialis)
- **Synergists**: Gluteus Maximus, Adductor Magnus, Hamstrings
- **Prescription**: 3 Sets &times; 8–12 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 150s &bull; **Tempo**: `3-0-1-0`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Position hips and lower back firmly against the backrest pad.
  2. Place feet shoulder-width apart in the center of the sled with toes turned slightly outward.
  3. Release safeties; lower sled smoothly until knees reach a 90° angle.
  4. Press through full foot (heels and midfoot) back up, stopping just short of locking knees.
- **Form Cues**: "Never round tailbone or hips off the seat" &bull; "Stop before knee lockout" &bull; "Knees track over toes".
- **Safety**: Always keep hands on side safety handles ready to lock sled if necessary.

---

### 3.2 Romanian Deadlift (RDL)
- **ID**: `romanian_deadlift` (`rdl`) &bull; **Category**: Legs &bull; **Equipment**: Olympic Barbell
- **Primary Agonist**: Hamstrings (Biceps Femoris, Semitendinosus)
- **Synergists**: Gluteus Maximus, Erector Spinae, Latissimus Dorsi
- **Prescription**: 2 Sets &times; 8–12 Reps &bull; **Rest**: 150s &bull; **Tempo**: `3-1-1-0`
- **Biomechanical Setup**:
  1. Stand tall holding barbell at hip level with shoulder-width overhand grip.
  2. Unlock knees slightly (maintain this soft knee bend fixed throughout).
  3. Push hips straight back as if closing a car door with glutes, letting bar skim down shins.
  4. Lower until feeling a deep hamstring stretch around mid-shin with a flat spine.
  5. Drive hips forward and squeeze glutes to return to standing.
- **Form Cues**: "Hip hinge (hips back), not a squat down" &bull; "Bar skims shins" &bull; "Flat spine".

---
